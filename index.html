Menu & allergy info

Breads. Sourdough is tangy and crusty, ciabatta is light with a crisp crust, seed loaf is hearty, and white and brown are the soft classics.

Fillings. Chicken comes grilled or as a creamy chicken mayo. Ham, bacon, roast beef, tuna and egg are all made fresh for your order.

Cheese. Cheddar and gouda are the classics, mozzarella is mild and creamy, feta is salty and tangy, and cream cheese is soft and smooth.

Sauces. Pick a mustard (Dijon, normal or wholegrain) or a pesto (basil pesto, olive tapenade or sundried tomato). There are also mayo, chutney and plain butter.

Onions are only added if you choose them: raw red, caramelised or pickled.

Allergens at a glance. Gluten: all breads. Dairy: cheese, butter and basil pesto. Egg: egg, mayo and chicken mayo. Fish: tuna, and sometimes olive tapenade. Nuts: pestos may contain nuts. Mustard: all mustards.

Everything is made in a kitchen that handles gluten, dairy, eggs, nuts, fish and mustard, so I can’t promise any sandwich is completely free of them. If you have an allergy, please tell me in the notes when you order.
